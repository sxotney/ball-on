# WPBSA 2024 Rulebook is the single source; explanations are paraphrased, not quoted

Every Question cites one rule in the WPBSA Official Rules (revised September 2024), stored in `docs/source/`. Explanations are written in our own words with a Rule Citation; the app never reproduces Rulebook text. The Rulebook is WPBSA copyright ("no part may be reproduced"), and paraphrasing keeps the app shareable with other trainee referees later. Looking up the cited rule in her own copy is also useful practice for the Learner. Scope within the Rulebook covers Snooker Sections 1–5, including the Six Reds variant (S3 R19). Four-handed Snooker (S3 R18), the Snooker Shootout and English Billiards are out of scope. Referee course material (positioning, procedure) is also out of scope until it can be added as a second, separately cited source.

## Consequences

- When WPBSA revises the rules, every Question citing a changed rule must be re-Reviewed. That's why each Question records its Rule Citation and the Rulebook edition.
- The repo is public and the app is served from GitHub Pages so Rani can share it with fellow trainee referees. That makes paraphrasing mandatory, not just a preference: the Rulebook PDF and its extracted text are gitignored and must never be committed.

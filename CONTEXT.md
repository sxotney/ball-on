# Ball On

**Ball On** is a revision app that keeps a trainee snooker referee sharp on rulings at the table, especially unusual eventualities. Every answer is grounded in the WPBSA Official Rules (revised September 2024).

## Learning material

**Question**:
One item in the Question Bank: a Scenario, a Recall question or a Judgement Call, always carrying a Rule Citation.
_Avoid_: Card, item, problem

**Scenario**:
A described table situation (with a Table Diagram where the layout matters) that the Learner must rule on by making the Ruling.
_Avoid_: Situation, case, example

**Judgement Call**:
A situation that turns on the referee's opinion (e.g. was the miss intentional, is a stalemate approaching). The Learner commits to a view, then reads the considerations. It isn't marked and isn't part of spaced repetition. A Scenario, by contrast, always states any judgement needed, so it has one Correct Ruling.
_Avoid_: Opinion question, grey-area scenario

**Recall question**:
A Question testing a single fact needed at speed during a frame (e.g. a penalty value, spotting order). It's answered quickly (a number, or a pick from a short fixed list) and marked as strictly as a Ruling.
_Avoid_: Fact question, flashcard

**Eventuality**:
An unusual or rule-combining situation (an "unless" clause, a sequence, an interaction between rules) — the main target of Scenarios.
_Avoid_: Edge case, corner case

**Table Diagram**:
A top-down picture of ball positions accompanying a Scenario.
_Avoid_: Layout, image

**Topic**:
A grouping of Questions by area of the rules (e.g. Free Ball, Foul and a Miss, Spotting Colours, Touching Ball, Six Reds).
_Avoid_: Category, chapter

**Question Bank**:
The full set of Reviewed Questions shipped with the app.
_Avoid_: Database, deck, pool

**Reviewed**:
A Question that a human (Carl or Rani) has checked against the Rulebook. Only Reviewed Questions reach the Learner.
_Avoid_: Approved, verified

**Draft**:
A Question not yet Reviewed. Learners never see Drafts; a Reviewer answers each one cold, before seeing the drafted Ruling.
_Avoid_: Unreviewed question, pending

**Disputed**:
A Draft where the Reviewer's own Ruling disagreed with the drafted one. It's settled against the Rulebook before it can become Reviewed, and it's often a good Eventuality in itself.
_Avoid_: Rejected, failed

**Flag**:
A Learner's mark that a Question looks wrong or unclear. Flags are shared with Rani, as lead Reviewer, to prompt re-review.
_Avoid_: Report, dispute

**Reviewer**:
A person trusted to mark a Question Reviewed: Rani (lead, and the receiver of Flags) or Carl.
_Avoid_: Admin, moderator, checker

**Incident** (v2):
A real situation the Learner met while refereeing, logged so it can become a Question once Reviewed.
_Avoid_: Log entry, note

## Revising

**Drill**:
A short session of about 10 Questions that the app picks for the Learner, mixing Questions due back with a few new ones. "Today's Drill" is the default way in.
_Avoid_: Quiz, test, session, round

**Topic practice**:
A session the Learner chooses, drawing Questions from one Topic only.
_Avoid_: Custom quiz, filter

**Due**:
A Question whose spaced-repetition interval has elapsed, so it's owed back to the Learner. Getting it wrong makes it due again soon; getting it right pushes it further out.
_Avoid_: Scheduled, pending, overdue

**Learner app** / **Review build**:
The app shared with Learners (Reviewed Questions only) / the separate app Reviewers use, which also contains Drafts.
_Avoid_: Prod/dev, public/private version

## Making a Ruling

**Ruling**:
The full structured answer to a Scenario, worked in referee order: the Call, the Penalty Points, the Options for the Incoming Player, and the state of the cue-ball.
_Avoid_: Answer, decision, verdict

**Correct Ruling**:
A Ruling where every part is right. Anything less counts as wrong for spaced repetition, though each part is shown marked.
_Avoid_: Pass, mostly right, partial credit

**Call**:
What the referee announces: none, FOUL, FOUL AND A MISS, FREE BALL, TOUCHING BALL, PENALTY (and combinations such as FOUL then FREE BALL).
_Avoid_: Shout, announcement

**Penalty Points**:
Points awarded to the Non-offender after an infringement; minimum four, otherwise as set by S3 R11.
_Avoid_: Foul points, penalty score

**Options**:
The choices open to the Incoming Player after a foul (play from the position left, ask the Offender to play again, have balls replaced to the original position, nominate a Free Ball).
The Learner always chooses from the same fixed Options List, so the list never hints at the answer.
_Avoid_: Choices, alternatives

**Options List**:
The single, Reviewer-approved master list of every Option the Rulebook allows after an infringement. The correct Options for any Scenario are a subset of it.
_Avoid_: Answer choices, menu

**Offender** / **Non-offender**:
The player who committed the infringement / their opponent. Used in preference to striker/opponent once an infringement has occurred.
_Avoid_: Fouler, victim

**Incoming Player**:
The player next to play after the striker's turn ends; after a foul this is the Non-offender.
_Avoid_: Next player, opponent

**Foul** vs **Penalty**:
As defined by the Rulebook (S2 R15): a Foul ends the Offender's turn; a Penalty awards points without changing the order of play (e.g. the non-striker infringing out of turn).
_Avoid_: Using these interchangeably

## Source

**Rulebook**:
The WPBSA Official Rules of the Games of Snooker and English Billiards, revised September 2024. The single source of truth.
_Avoid_: The rules, the book, regulations

**Rule Citation**:
A reference to the Rulebook in the form `S3 R14(d)(ii)` (Section, Rule, paragraph, sub-paragraph).
_Avoid_: Reference, source, link

**Learner**:
A trainee referee using the app to revise. Rani first, and other trainee referees she shares it with.
_Avoid_: User, student, player (a Player is someone at the table)
